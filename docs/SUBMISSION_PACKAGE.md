### Current software-progress narration (safe before live validation)

> SHOR is a local speech reliability lab for Indian-language applications. The
> workbench accepts WAV audio and keeps synthetic noise separate from real
> recording conditions. In preview mode, the application generates original,
> degraded and recovered audio without inventing transcription scores. Its
> recovery controller uses acoustic indicators rather than the reference answer,
> and records why it chose filtering or pass-through. Word alignment and critical
> term diagnostics are implemented for real transcripts once available. We can
> export evidence, filter history and delete stored recordings. These are software
> capabilities, not proof of improved speech recognition. Live Gnani access and a
> reviewed, consented held-out speech benchmark remain to be validated.
